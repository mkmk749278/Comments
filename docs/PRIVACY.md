# Privacy policy: Comments

*Last updated: 29 October 2026*

**Comments** is a private tool used only by the owner of two YouTube channels, **Built on Bharat** (@builtonbharat)
and **Be Practical with Kishore** (@bepracticalwithkishore), to read the comments on those channels' own videos and
reply to them. It has no other users.

## What it accesses
Through the YouTube Data API, with the channel owner's sign-in, it:
- reads public comments and replies on the two channels' videos, and comments held for review;
- posts replies that the channel owner has approved, as the channel;
- hides comments or blocks commenters on those channels when the owner decides to (spam, scams, abuse).

It does not access any other Google data, any other channel, or any viewer's account.

## What it keeps
- A log of which comments were handled and how (comment id, video id, commenter's public display name, the action
  taken, the reply id). All of this is already public on YouTube.
- Corrections viewers point out about our films, to fix our future work.

It does not collect email addresses, phone numbers, passwords or any private information about commenters, and
never asks commenters for any. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers or used for anything except replying to
comments on our own channels.

## Sign-in tokens
The channel owner's sign-in token is stored only as a private secret in the owner's own cloud environment, never in
this repository. The owner can withdraw access at any time at myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party
connections.

## Google API Services
Use of information received from Google APIs follows the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including
the Limited Use requirements, and the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms).
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
